## Coordination-specific release gates

1. Exact pinned CI must build **all 45 addons against the same 4.1.74 candidate core SHA** with a verified manifest and bytecode, not rely on separately green addon PRs.
2. The [two-boot full-stack smoke](https://github.com/wickidcow/Slimefun-Legacy/actions/workflows/paper-26.3-full-stack.yml) must pass for Paper 1.21.11, Paper 26.2 and Paper 26.3, and the experimental Purpur 26.3 line. Confirm addon startup and plugin dependency order.
3. Confirm the combined Networks 1.0.54 / FluffyMachines 26.2.16 version pairing can retrieve deposited metadata/PDC-rich items, honor real barrel output slots, and preserve combined source+network quantity before and after a server restart. Existing individual native tests do **not** alone establish these joint behaviors.
4. Preserve Quantum, InfinityExpansion2, backpack and vanilla-inventory source identities, menus, transfer priority, item counts, old-world serialization and saved-player inventories.
5. A representative AlbionMC world clone must confirm that no dupe or item loss occurs under repeated input/output, and provide comparable `/sf tick top` samples to establish real-world performance changes.
6. Before release publication, merge/tag and verify independently released Networks **v1.0.54** and FluffyMachines **v26.2.16** JAR digests. Only then approve a final exact-pinned manifest for the next versioned Slimefun core and new addon ZIP. Use normal stop/backup/restart deployment; never `/reload`.
